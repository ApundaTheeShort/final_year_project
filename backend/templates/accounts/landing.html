About Us

We make produce transport easier for both farmers and transporters.

Farmers can choose pickup and dropoff locations on the map, see the transport price before paying, and follow the trip until the goods are delivered.

Transporters can keep their vehicle details up to date, view available jobs that match their vehicle, and manage the journey from pickup to delivery in one place.

How It Works

The process is simple for both the farmer booking the trip and the transporter handling it.

Step 1

Book and pay

The farmer creates the booking, sees the transport amount, and pays with M-Pesa.

Step 2

Transporter accepts

The transporter sees the request, accepts the booking, and heads to pickup.

Step 3

Deliver and confirm

The goods are delivered and the farmer can see the delivery progress along the way.
